Featured Work

Goldfish Tank MR Art

From the screen, into reality.

Goldfish leap out of a tank shown on a PC screen and swim through mixed reality on Meta Quest. I built it to create the moment where what lives inside a screen crosses over into the real room — at events and demos.

  • Mixed Reality
  • WebXR
  • Meta Quest
  • Interactive Art
Try it on PC ↗ See how it works
How to try it
  1. Open the work's URL on a PC or Mac
  2. Visit the URL shown on screen using the Meta Quest browser
  3. Entering MR mode on Quest connects to the PC automatically
  4. Press the start button on the PC to begin
  5. The first time you point at the tank with your index finger, hold still a little longer to avoid misdetection

This experience uses both a PC and a Meta Quest. On a PC alone you can still see the tank itself.

Shibapapa is a creator making XR content with AI.
